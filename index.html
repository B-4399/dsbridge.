Weekly Immigrational Updates And Policies for TRP Issuance or Renewal

  • As of 22 October, 2025 applicant for TRP or visa must schedule an appointment
    through the official portal of MIGRIS meaning no vfs(external service provider)
    handle booking for Lithuanian visa/residence permits.
  • The following countries have been removed as external service providers: UAE, Argentina, Tajikistan and Venezuela.
  • The following countries have been added: Ghana, Vietnam, Bosnia, Herzogovina and zimbabwe

Documents for TRP issuance

  • Health insurance
  • Bank statement
  • Police clearance Certificate
  • Admission letter(for first time students)
  • Mediation number from University
  • Passport copy with atleast 2years validity
